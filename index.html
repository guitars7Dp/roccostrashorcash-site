Inside the app

See what goes into the verdict.

Enter offer details like pay, mileage, and estimated time. See whether the offer meets your hourly pay target after fuel costs, with estimated hourly earnings before and after fuel. Fuel costs are estimated using your vehicle’s MPG and the gas price you enter in Settings.
