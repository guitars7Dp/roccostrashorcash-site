Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Rocco’s Trash or Cash handles information in its iPhone app and on its beta signup website.

Settings and order history

Rocco uses the offer details and vehicle settings you enter to estimate hourly pay after fuel. The app saves settings, such as your hourly target, vehicle MPG, and fuel price, in its local storage. The order log stores the orders you choose to log in the app’s local storage.

Voice input

If you use Siri or Tap to Speak, Apple’s Siri or speech recognition services process what you say so Rocco can read the offer details. How Apple processes voice input can depend on your device, settings, and the feature you use. You can also enter offers manually.

Apple’s Siri and Dictation privacy information

Feedback and diagnostics

If you send feedback, the message goes to the developer so they can respond and troubleshoot. Rocco includes the app version and iOS version in the draft email. You can choose to include recent diagnostics, which may contain spoken offer details or connected device names. You can review and edit the message before sending it. The feedback email does not attach an audio recording.

Beta signup and questionnaires

If you apply through the website, the form sends your name, email address, delivery platform selections, and testing commitment to the developer through Formspree. We use application information to manage participation and send TestFlight invitations. If you participate, we use questionnaire responses to gather feedback and track completion of the test program.

Privacy questions and requests

For privacy questions or to request access, correction, or deletion of information you’ve sent us, email ds3delivers@gmail.com.
