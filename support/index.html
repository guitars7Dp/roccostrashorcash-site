Contact us

Email ds3delivers@gmail.com or open Rocco and tap Settings → Send Feedback. If something went wrong, let us know what you were trying to do and what happened.

How do I enter an offer by voice?

Open Rocco’s Settings → Tutorial for the Siri walkthrough. You can also open Rocco and tap Tap to Speak. If your iPhone asks for microphone or speech recognition access, allow it to use voice entry. You can always enter the offer manually.

How does Rocco estimate pay after fuel?

Rocco uses the offer amount, estimated time and mileage, your vehicle’s MPG, and the fuel price you enter. Expand More Details to add estimated return miles if you expect to drive back. Rocco compares the resulting hourly estimate with the target you set in Settings.

Where can I find a saved order?

Tap Log It on the verdict for an order you took. To see saved orders, open Settings → Order Log.
